## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static site generation), chosen by the owner on 2026-11-10. It replaces the current WordPress site (GeneratePress theme, hosted on O2Switch, Apache). The site must keep working as static files on that hosting. Old URLs (`/realisations/{catégorie}/{slug}.html` and the WordPress pages) need 301 redirects.

## Users

The owner kept the current audience mix unchanged ("on change rien, juste refait pareil"):
- broadcasters and platforms commissioning reportages and documentaries (France Télévisions is the proven case);
- institutions, public bodies and organisations commissioning reportages about their own projects (for example the Ville d'Argenteuil and the Société Française de Médecine Légale);
- publishers and press titles (investigations and books);
- partners of the event « Quelle info demain ? ».

Inferred situation: someone evaluating a production company at their desk who wants to judge the work, then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Wallraff Contents is a Paris production company (SAS, Paris 10e) that produces TV reportages and documentaries, press investigations, books, events and social-media content. The site exists to show the work and turn a visitor into a project enquiry. Success means a qualified contact through the form, by phone or by email.

## Positioning

The company was founded by Frédéric Crotta, a former grand reporter at France Télévisions, and its signature is "100 % éditorial": the editorial rigour of investigative journalism applied to every format (television, events, social media, press, books). The positioning stays as it is today and is not rewritten in this redesign.

## Operating Context

- Work is judged on films (YouTube trailers and excerpts), stills with burned-in subtitles, magazine spreads and book covers.
- The founder's network of journalists, camera operators and editors is part of the offer.
- The company co-organises the event « Quelle info demain ? » (Luxeuil-les-Bains).
- The founder also exhibits his photographs (prints in Plexi and Alu formats).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The content (texts, works, pages) is kept. Only typos and factual inconsistencies are corrected, never the claims.
- Contact details are inconsistent on the current site (3 emails, 2 phone numbers). The owner must confirm the official ones; until then the site uses production@wallraff.fr and 06 07 67 85 76, the details shown in the footer and its mailto.
- The contact form needs a third-party endpoint because the site is static (Web3Forms, Formspree or similar). **Open decision:** which service, and its access key.
- **Open decisions:** whether the gallery prints are for sale (no price on the current site), and which "partners" logos are real clients.
- French only.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Wallraff Contents.
- Logo: a red square with a white "W", next to the black "WALLRAFF" wordmark and "CONTENTS" in red. It is kept.
- Visual direction pinned by the owner: "magazine d'enquête". Strong headlines; black, white and red; the logo as a press stamp at the centre; burned-in subtitles used as a motif.

## Product Principles

1. The work proves the claim: always show a film, a spread or a book before saying anything about it.
2. Journalistic precision in every detail: dates, broadcasters, durations and credits are stated, not implied.
3. One clear way in: every page leads to a single way to start a project.
4. Nothing invented: no fabricated proof, figures or clients.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA: contrast, keyboard, focus, alt text and heading structure.
- Videos are not autoplayed with sound.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
