## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM: GeneratePress + default Gutenberg blocks template; swap the photos and an unrelated business could use it unchanged. Only the logo and real stills are Wallraff's own. Template-isms: default page-title H1 above the hero, image-less charcoal box, two rows of 1/2/3 single-word pillars, four dimmed cover blocks with paragraph + 2 outline buttons, heterogeneous logo wall (incl. an SEO consultant), 3D book/laptop mockups, stock tablet photo on contact, system font everywhere, agency boilerplate copy.

Deterministic scan: 5 primary findings across 7 scans. 4x line-length (~130-140 chars/line in homepage cover blocks and the "La guerre des clans" intro), 1x low-contrast (contact "Envoyer" button #ff000c on white = 4.0:1, also on hover; missed by the LLM review). Advisories: 3 stragglers, unbalanced mobile H1. No false positives. Detector missed white-on-photo text in cover blocks: pixel sampling shows up to 47% of background below 4.5:1 behind the "Édition, Presse" paragraph (min 3.95:1), which the LLM review also caught.

Overlay: not available (no user-visible browser).

## Overall Impression
Technically sound (FCP <400ms, 0 console errors, 0 broken internal links) but positioned as a generic "producteur de contenus" when the real material is investigative documentary by an ex-France TV grand reporter. Biggest opportunity: real footage and founder pedigree in the first viewport; films as the product.
